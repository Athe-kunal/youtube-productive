# Privacy Policy — YouTube Intent Filter

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Data Collection
This extension does not collect, transmit, or upload any user data.

## Data Storage
The following data is stored locally in your browser only:
- Your intent description (what you want to see)
- The computed embedding vector
- A cache of video similarity scores
- Your keyword, schedule, and hardcore-mode settings
- Daily counts of videos filtered and unhidden (numbers only, no titles), kept for 90 days

All data remains on your device and is never sent to external servers.

## Model
The embedding model (~34 MB) is bundled inside the extension package. The extension makes no
network requests of its own and only has access to youtube.com pages; all inference runs locally.
